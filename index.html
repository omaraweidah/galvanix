Product Specifications

100mm Foam-Filled Interlocking Slat

🔒
Material: Hot-dip galvanized steel with 50-micron coating
Thickness: 1.50mm at interlocking joint
Insulation: Injected polyurethane foam for thermal insulation
Security: Unique zigzag interlock design for anti-theft protection
Weight: 12.8 kg/m²
Finish: Polyester electrostatic powder paint, UV resistant for 10 years
Customization: Dual-tone options available

80mm Single-Wall Slat

🛡️
Material: Galvanized steel with 120 zinc layer
Weight: 12.5 kg/m² (anti-theft qualified)
Coating: Electrostatic polyester coating
Durability: Resistant to scratching, rust, and UV radiation
Design: Contoured design for beauty and strength
Dimensions: Up to 6m width × 6m height
Applications: Large garages, retail spaces, industrial units
