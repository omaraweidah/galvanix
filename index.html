Company Profile

World's First Foam-Filled Interlocking Slats

GALVANIX is the world's first and only manufacturer of 100mm foam-filled slats featuring a newly engineered interlocking system. As a knowledge-based company, we set new industry standards in the electric shutter market by combining advanced engineering, innovative design, and state-of-the-art manufacturing technologies.

We utilize cutting-edge techniques and premium raw materials, making us a trusted choice for industrial, residential, and commercial projects across Iran and international markets. GALVANIX operates active agencies in the UAE and Oman, ensuring consistent accessibility and service.
